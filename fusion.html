Our generation's moonshot: a star in a bottle.

the fuel is in seawater: enough for everyone, forever

Except a star vaporizes every bottle we own.

one hundred million degrees, six times hotter than the sun's core

But a star's fuel is charged particles, and charged particles obey magnetic fields.

a particle spirals around a field line like a bead on a wire: free to slide along it, unable to leave it

So we loop those field lines into a ring: a bottle with walls of pure magnetism.

the fuel rides the lines around and around. it never touches metal

One catch: the cage must twist like a corkscrew, or the star slips out.
